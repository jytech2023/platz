## Greeting from the CEO

We celebrated our 30th anniversary in July 2022, and to date, we have sold about 700,000 beds, which have been used in homes and about 10,000 nursing homes and hospitals throughout Japan.

As a listed company, we are wholly dedicated to respond to our clients, shareholders, and customers’ expectations and to earn their trust by providing services and products, improve the quality of all our business activities, and raise the value of our company

We ask that you continue to provide us with guidance and support.
